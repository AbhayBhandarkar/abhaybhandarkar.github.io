Abhay Bhandarkar

I'm currently a Business Guest at Microsoft Research India working on Computer Vision in applications for design and creativity and am fortunate to be supervised by Dr. Vineeth N Balasubramanian. I am pursuing my Bachelor's in Computer Science at Ramaiah Institute of Technology in Bangalore, India, and I work on applied computer vision, multimodal AI, and explainability in deep learning systems.

My research interests span applied computer vision, perception and interpretation, explainability, and multimodality. I'm particularly fascinated by how we can make AI systems more interpretable and trustworthy, especially in high-stakes domains like autonomous systems, medical imaging and aerospace.

I've worked on projects ranging from medical image segmentation with efficient vision transformers to defense surveillance systems using deep learning, and I'm passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world applications.

Research

I'm interested in computer vision, deep learning, multimodal AI, and explainability. My work focuses on developing efficient and interpretable models for real-world applications, particularly in medical imaging and aerospace domains. Some papers are highlighted.

Investigating Thematic Patterns and User Preferences in LLM Interactions using BERTopic
Abhay Bhandarkar, Gaurav Mishra, Khushi Juchani, Harsh Singhal
arXiv, 2025
arXiv / PDF

Applies BERTopic to the LMSYS-Chat-1M dataset to uncover thematic patterns in how users interact with and evaluate competing large language models, revealing insights into human preferences for LLM outputs.

SIMHA: Sustainable Integrated Microbial and Hydrogen Fuel Cell Architecture
Abhay Bhandarkar, et al.
IEEE SPACE, 2025

A novel sustainable energy architecture integrating microbial fuel cells with hydrogen fuel cells for space and terrestrial applications. Also filed as a patent.

Advancements in Defense Surveillance: Integrating Deep Learning and Enhanced Materials in CubeSAT Systems
Abhay Bhandarkar, Riddhi Rai, Sushma Bylaiah
IEEE SPACE, 2024

Integrating state-of-the-art deep learning architectures with advanced materials science for next-generation CubeSAT-based defense surveillance systems.

TotCareBot: A Comprehensive Approach towards Enhancing Early Childhood Education through Robotics
Manjula R Chougala, Sushma Bylaiah, Abhay Bhandarkar, Akshata S Bhayyar, P N Pavitra
I4C, 2024

A comprehensive robotic system designed to enhance early childhood education through interactive and engaging learning experiences.

Unmasking the AI Hand: A Machine Learning Approach to Deciphering Authorship
Abhay Bhandarkar, Vishwachetan D
IEEE INOCON, 2024

A machine learning framework for detecting AI-generated text and distinguishing between human and machine authorship in written content.

Minds and Materials in Flight: The Dynamic Duo of Smart Materials and Machine Learning in Aerospace Evolution
Abhay Bhandarkar, Dr. Nagaraju Kottam, Vishwachetan D
ICAMES, 2023

Exploring the synergistic integration of smart materials and machine learning for advancing aerospace engineering and design.
